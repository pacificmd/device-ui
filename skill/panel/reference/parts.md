# Parts

Every part, with the forms it comes in. A form is the value on its tag, as `d-key="chiclet"`. Open a part's group for its attributes and an example. Generated by `pnpm catalogue`.

## [Keys](parts/keys.md)

- `d-key`: Something you press and it comes back. Forms: arcade, flush, tact, mushroom, type, piano, membrane, tab, chiclet, mech, face, pill, mat, etched, side, sculpt, soft, sensor, slab, tile, drum, edge, plunger, touch, step, switch, scissor, screen, ring, bumper, guide, lamp, pusher.
- `d-switch`: Something you throw from one position to another. Forms: seesaw, toggle, spring, throw, rocker, key, bar, flick, blade.

## [Rotary](parts/rotary.md)

- `d-knob`: Something you grip and turn. Forms: skirt, cap, collet, flared, stem, bar, pointer, jog, knurled, wing, ribbed, flange, selector, tension, hat.

## [Switches](parts/switches.md)
